2. Add Your Stops

Paste your whole stop list at once (one address per line), or add stops one at a time below. The order you enter is the order the route will drive.

Set this once. Any stop you type without a city — including cross streets like Cavendish/Westminster — will automatically get this city added, and the slash will be turned into "&" so Google Maps reads it as an intersection.

3. Check the Order

First stop is where the route starts. Last stop is where it ends. Use the arrows to reorder, the 📍 to pin the exact side of the street, or the X to remove a stop.
